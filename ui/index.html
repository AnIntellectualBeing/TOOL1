Good to know

  • Sound from the phone needs Android 11 or newer.
  • Using the phone's camera needs Android 12 or newer. It opens in a PhoneMirror window; making it show up as a webcam in Zoom or Teams on Windows isn't done yet.
  • Apps like Netflix and some banking apps show a black screen. That's the app's choice to protect its content.
  • Wireless debugging turns itself off when the phone restarts or changes Wi-Fi. If a phone won't connect, turn it on again.

Help & limits

Honest limits

  • iPhone can't be controlled from the PC. Apple doesn't allow it. You can see and hear it.
  • Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. Some public, hotel, school or office Wi-Fi blocks devices from seeing each other. Your phone's hotspot works: connect the PC to it.
  • Protected apps show a black screen (Netflix, some banking apps). That's the app's choice.
  • Android sound needs Android 11+. Wireless pairing also needs Android 11+; older phones work with a USB cable.
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose “More info” → “Run anyway”.
  • The phone's picture opens in its own window for now.

Something's not working?

My iPhone doesn't see the PC

Check both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network). In Settings → Network & firewall, press Fix firewall. If Windows calls your Wi-Fi “Public”, either set it to Private in Windows Settings or press Allow on this network. Turning the iPhone's Wi-Fi off and on also helps.

The Android QR code doesn't work

Make sure you tapped Pair device with QR code (inside Wireless debugging), not the camera app. Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. If it still fails, use Pair with a code instead, or a USB cable.

“Your phone and PC are on different Wi-Fi networks”

Many routers have a separate 5 GHz, 2.4 GHz or guest network. Put both devices on the same one. Mesh systems and office networks sometimes separate devices; a phone hotspot is the quick fix.

I can see the Android screen but can't click

Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO: turn on USB debugging (Security settings) in Developer options and restart the phone. OPPO/Realme: turn on Disable permission monitoring.

No sound from Android

Sound needs Android 11 or newer. On Android 11, unlock the phone before connecting.

The picture is black or stutters

Try a lower quality preset. For iPhone, try Settings → Video output → Compatible. For protected apps a black picture is expected.

Privacy

PhoneMirror has no account, no ads, no telemetry and no analytics. It doesn't use the internet: the picture and sound go straight from your phone to your PC over your local network. The one exception is Cast videos from apps (off unless you turn it on): then your PC fetches that video from YouTube itself, as your iPhone asked.
